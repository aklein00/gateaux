## Art Style Guidelines

### Overall Style
- **Warm and inviting café aesthetic**
- **Hand-drawn or illustrated style** (not photorealistic)
- **Consistent color palette**: Warm browns, creams, pastels
- **Slightly cartoonish** but not childish

### Customer Portraits
- Show personality through facial expressions
- Include relevant accessories (tourist camera, hipster glasses, business suit, etc.)
- Circular format with soft edges

### Cat Characters
- Anthropomorphic but still clearly cats
- Show personality through posture and accessories
- Each should be instantly distinguishable

### Cakes
- Top-down or 3/4 view
- Show freshness through:
  - Fresh: Glossy, vibrant colors
  - Day-old: Slightly duller, less perfect
  - Stale: Cracked, dull, maybe some mold spots

### Icons
- Simple, clear, readable at small size
- Consistent line weight
- Match the warm color palette
