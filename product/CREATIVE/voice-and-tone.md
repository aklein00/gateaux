# Voice and tone

Gateaux should sound like a good café on a Tuesday: quick, a little rude, actually kind.

Duolingo’s trick is not the owl. It is that the app talks like a friend who is slightly too pleased with a pun. We want that, in a patisserie, with cats who have opinions.

## The sentence we are writing toward

“Say it like you would to the person behind the counter, not like you would on an exam.”

## Registers

| Register | When | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Colloquial default | UI, Marcel, first lessons | “Hey — the usual?” |
| Polite on purpose | Amélie lessons, labeled as fancy | “How may I help you?” is a *costume*, not the narrator |
| Stressed | Café, rush | Short. No complete sentences required. |
| Extra | Bisou / Gaston | Flirt or roast. PG-13. Never creepy, never cruel to the player. |

The **narrator** (buttons, titles, empty states) is Marcel-adjacent: casual, short, no corporate warmth.

## Words we use

yeah, hey, the usual, nailed it, not quite, one more, go on, fair, ouch, nice, try that again, you’re good

## Words we do not use (in UI)

utilize, commence, please select, lesson complete, check, submit, well done!, let’s get started, continue your journey, unlock your potential, recipe unlock (unless a cake actually unlocks)

“Correct!” is a grade. Prefer “That’s the one.” / “Marcel would say it like that.” / “Ok wow, look at you.”

## Character voices (one line each)

Use these as a lint test. If a line could be said by any of them, it is too bland.

- **Amélie** — precise, a little vain, secretly nice. “Almost. The *vous* matters, dear.”
- **Marcel** — alley-cat, warm. “Nah. Try the one you’d actually say.”
- **Café** — drowning, funny. “Wrong. Also the milk is scorching. It’s *this*.”
- **Bisou** — theatrical, not thirsty. “That was not it, and I am choosing to forgive you.”
- **Gaston** — bored, then helpful. “No. Obviously. Here.”

## Lesson titles

Bad: Formal Greetings. Taking Orders. Lesson 1.
Good: How locals say hi. The usual. When they’re already mad.

## Length

- Button: 1–3 words
- Feedback: 1 sentence
- Tutorial: 1 sentence or nothing
- Correction: 1–2 sentences, then the right phrase, big

## Humor rules (from the GDD, still law)

PG-13. Tease the situation, not the player’s intelligence. Cats can roast each other. Wordplay is welcome. If a slang line needs a footnote to be safe, cut it.
