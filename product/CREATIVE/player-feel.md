# Player feel

This is the creative-manager document. If status.md is “is it working?”, this is “does it feel like *us*?”

## The feeling we owe

Five minutes. A small win. A cat with a personality. A phrase you would actually use when the barista looks at you.

Not: a bakery you have to manage before you are allowed to learn. Not: a timed exam. Not: a skill tree.

**Reference mix**

| Steal | From | Not from |
|---|---|---|
| Speed to first question, streaks as applause, character as co-star | Duolingo | Grammar trees, hearts-as-punishment (yet) |
| Warm room, staff you like, something in the case | Cozy café fiction | Full restaurant sim |
| Juice on tap (press, pop, short celebration) | Polished mobile games | Extra currencies, chunky Clash frames |

We already decided the palette stays muted café, not saturated cartoon. Feel is pacing and voice, not more color.

## Feel targets (observable)

A screenshot or a 30-second recording should show:

1. A question on screen within ~15 seconds of tapping Start (returning player: even faster).
2. The first phrase is something you’d say to a friend (`Salut`, `¿Qué onda?`), not a hotel concierge line.
3. The teacher is looking at you. Success makes *them* react, not just a green banner.
4. No clock running unless you opted into chaos.
5. One primary action. Not Bake + Recipe Book + New Customer + Mute all shouting.

## Feel anti-targets

- Tutorial with four icons explaining an economy
- Rarity badges before you have baked once
- “CHECK” as the emotional climax of a question
- English in the customer’s mouth as the default
- Milestone gifts that are empty boxes
- The word “Loading…”

## Loop, as it should feel

```
tap Start
  → a cat goes “hey”
  → you answer 4–5 tiny things
  → a cake appears (delight, not inventory)
  → maybe someone walks in and you use one line
  → you’re done, or you do one more
```

The display case is a trophy shelf you glance at. It is not the sport.

## Why it doesn’t feel great today

We built the trophy shelf, the recipe book, the rarity ladder, and the decay sim around a single multiple-choice exam that starts in formal register. The *systems* are heavier than the *joke*.

Duolingo feels light because the joke (the characters, the copy, the next card) is heavier than the systems. Flip that ratio.

## Creative QA questions (run after any UI change)

1. Would Marcel say this button label out loud?
2. Can I skip this screen and still learn?
3. Am I being graded or am I being talked to?
4. Did a cat get a moment, or only a progress bar?

If 3 of 4 fail, it is not done, even if it functions.
